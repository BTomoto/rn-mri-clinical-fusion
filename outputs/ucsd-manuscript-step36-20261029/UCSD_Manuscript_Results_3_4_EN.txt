For the prespecified primary comparison, the Fusion-minus-M1 ROC AUC difference was 0.110 (95% paired patient-bootstrap percentile interval, 0.021 to 0.214; Table 3). The treatment-related-change AP difference was 0.152 (0.026 to 0.268). Both intervals lay above zero under the conditional bootstrap analysis. Probability-error differences also favored Fusion in their point estimates: the Brier-score difference was -0.019 (-0.040 to 0.002), and the logarithmic-loss difference was -0.037 (-0.107 to 0.039). However, both probability-error intervals included zero, leaving these improvements uncertain. ROC AUC was the sole primary incremental metric; AP, Brier score and logarithmic loss were auxiliary summaries. Differences were calculated from the unrounded model metrics, rather than from the displayed three-decimal values. All contrasts used the same 182 examinations, and each bootstrap attempt applied the same resampled patient groups to both models. The resulting intervals described uncertainty in the paired differences conditional on the saved OOF predictions. They were not obtained by subtracting separate model interval bounds and did not include full retraining or partition variability. The primary result supported an AUC increment from adding the eight MRI features to the fixed clinical model within this cohort, while the probability-error contrasts remained uncertain.
