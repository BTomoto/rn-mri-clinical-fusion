This secondary analysis of public UCSD-PTGBM data supports an incremental MRI signal for distinguishing residual or recurrent tumor from treatment-related change after treatment for high-grade glioma. In 182 examinations from 135 patients, the fixed clinical-MRI fusion model achieved an out-of-fold AUC of 0.782, exceeding the expanded clinical model by 0.110, with a conditional paired 95% percentile interval of 0.021 to 0.214. The increment remained positive across 20 repeated patient-grouped partitions, while its presence and magnitude were uncertain in the single-scan analysis. Limited recognition of treatment-related change at the fixed threshold and unresolved improvements in probability error constrain clinical interpretation. The reproducible workflow provides a computational basis for evaluating low-dimensional fusion, subject to inherited diagnoses and the supplied derived images. Independent diagnostic evaluation and assessment of clinically justified decisions remain necessary to determine whether the observed increment generalizes and can support care.
