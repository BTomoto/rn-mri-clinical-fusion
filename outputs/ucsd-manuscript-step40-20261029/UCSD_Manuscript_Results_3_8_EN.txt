Selecting the smallest available numeric session per patient retained 135 examinations from 135 patients, including 113 tumor and 22 treatment-related-change examinations. This selection did not establish the true first clinical visit. OOF AUCs were 0.501 for M0, 0.609 for M1, 0.729 for MRI and 0.730 for Fusion (95% patient-bootstrap percentile interval for Fusion, 0.606 to 0.833; Supplementary Table S6 and Figure S2A). The Fusion-minus-M1 AUC difference was 0.120, with a paired interval of -0.015 to 0.252 that included zero (Supplementary Table S7). The auxiliary AP, Brier-score and logarithmic-loss difference intervals also included zero. At the fixed threshold, Fusion recognized 109/113 tumor examinations (96.5%) and 2/22 treatment-related-change examinations (9.1%; Figure S2B). Its descriptive calibration slope was 0.539, without subsequent recalibration. The selected analysis differed from the primary 182-examination analysis in examination composition, class distribution and training data. Consequently, the difference between their AUC estimates could not be attributed solely to removing repeated scans. Incremental discrimination remained uncertain in the single-scan analysis, which was retained as a sensitivity analysis without replacing the primary comparison.
