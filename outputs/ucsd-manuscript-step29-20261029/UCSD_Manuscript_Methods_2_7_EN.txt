Performance was calculated at the examination level by pooling OOF probabilities from all five folds. The primary incremental comparison was the Fusion-minus-M1 difference in area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (ROC AUC), evaluated on the same examinations (Equation 8). We also reported ROC AUC for each model, average precision (AP) for each diagnostic class, Brier score and logarithmic loss. Treatment-related-change AP used treatment-related change as the positive class and one minus the tumor probability as its score. AP was computed from precision weighted by increments in recall. Brier score and logarithmic loss summarized probability error, with lower values indicating better performance. At the fixed threshold of 0.5, probabilities at least 0.5 were classified as tumor. Confusion counts, tumor sensitivity, recognition of treatment-related change, precision for each class, balanced accuracy and overall accuracy were calculated without threshold optimization.

Uncertainty was summarized using 2000 patient-cluster bootstrap attempts with random seed 20261030. Each attempt sampled 135 patient groups with replacement and retained all available examinations for each selected group, including repeated copies when a group was sampled more than once. All models used identical resampled examination indices, enabling paired metric differences. The saved OOF probabilities remained fixed; prediction models and their preprocessing were not refitted within resampling. Attempts containing only one diagnostic class were ineligible and were not replaced by additional draws. For ROC AUC, treatment-related-change AP, Brier score and logarithmic loss, and their paired differences, 95% percentile intervals were defined by the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of eligible bootstrap values. These intervals represented patient-sampling uncertainty conditional on the saved OOF predictions. They did not capture the full variability of retraining the models or choosing another fold partition. Metric differences were retained in their stated directions, so a negative Brier-score difference favored Fusion in the Fusion-minus-M1 comparison.
