All 20 prespecified repetitions completed the patient-grouped five-fold validation without failed fold-model fits. Across repetitions, median AUCs were 0.556 for M0, 0.667 for M1, 0.775 for MRI and 0.784 for Fusion (Supplementary Table S5A; Figure 3A). Fusion had first and third AUC quartiles of 0.775 and 0.795, with an observed range of 0.759 to 0.809. The median within-repetition Fusion-minus-M1 AUC difference was 0.125, with a range of 0.103 to 0.150; all 20 differences were positive (Supplementary Table S5B; Figure 3B). AP, Brier-score and logarithmic-loss differences also favored Fusion in all 20 repetitions. These sign counts described the observed partitions and did not replace the primary paired bootstrap intervals. Because the same patients were reused, the repetitions were not independent validation cohorts. Medians, quartiles and ranges summarized sensitivity to partitioning and were not confidence intervals. The first repetition reproduced the original partition and predictions, and its Fusion AUC of 0.782 remained the primary estimate rather than the maximum observed across repetitions.
