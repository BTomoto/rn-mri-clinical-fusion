Further work should prioritize independently adjudicated examination diagnoses and documented annotation procedures, including the information available to annotators. Acquisition metadata and spatial scales should be verified, with preprocessing specified before evaluation. The locked clinical and fusion pipelines should be evaluated in an independent cohort representing the intended population, with sample size planned for precise performance estimation (15,16). Assessment should give particular attention to treatment-related-change recognition and calibration, alongside discrimination and probability error. Any clinical-utility study would additionally require a defined action and justified thresholds. These studies would provide evidence on whether the observed imaging increment persists beyond the present dataset.
