At the examination level, median age in the imaging cohort was 56.5 years [49.0, 66.0], and male patients contributed 132 examinations (72.5%; Table 1). Age, sex and other treatment before imaging were complete. Among 146 examinations with a known interval since the initial event, the median was 154.0 days [40.5, 359.5]. The corresponding medians were 96.0 days for tumor examinations and 346.0 days for treatment-related-change examinations. Intervals were missing for 36 examinations (19.8%), comprising 33 tumor and three treatment-related-change examinations. Other treatment before imaging was recorded for 25 examinations (13.7%). In the group without imaging coverage, median age was 53.5 years [45.0, 62.0], 60.7% of examinations were from male patients and 82.1% carried tumor labels, compared with 77.5% in the imaging cohort (Supplementary Table S1). These summaries used examination records, with interval distributions restricted to observed values before imputation. Bracketed values indicate the first and third quartiles. No between-group significance tests were performed.
