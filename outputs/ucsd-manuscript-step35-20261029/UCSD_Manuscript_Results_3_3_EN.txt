Each of the four fixed models provided an OOF probability for all 182 examinations under the same patient-grouped five-fold partition. The pooled ROC AUC was 0.526 for M0 (95% patient-bootstrap percentile interval, 0.404 to 0.644), 0.672 for M1 (0.553 to 0.778), 0.762 for MRI (0.683 to 0.838) and 0.782 for Fusion (0.698 to 0.859; Table 2 and Figure 2A). Treatment-related-change AP was 0.261, 0.354, 0.444 and 0.505 for M0, M1, MRI and Fusion, respectively, against a treatment-related-change prevalence of 0.225 in this cohort (Figure 2B). The corresponding Brier scores were 0.177, 0.163, 0.151 and 0.144. Fusion had the largest AUC and treatment-related-change AP point estimates and the lowest Brier score among the four models in this partition. The intervals summarized patient-sampling uncertainty conditional on the saved OOF probabilities; they did not include the full variability of model retraining or alternative fold assignments. These results represent internal validation in the same imaging cohort. The prespecified paired Fusion-minus-M1 comparison is reported separately, because the ordering of model-specific point estimates alone does not quantify the uncertainty in their difference.
