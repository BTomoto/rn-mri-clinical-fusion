The source cohort contained 243 examinations from 178 patients, including 192 examinations labeled as tumor and 51 labeled as treatment-related change (Figure 1). Identifier quarantine removed five examinations from two patients, leaving 238 examinations from 176 patients in the clinical cohort. The retained cohort contained 187 tumor and 51 treatment-related-change examinations. Complete FLAIR, postcontrast T1-weighted and annotation-mask inputs were available for 182 examinations from 135 patients, representing 76.5% of the retained clinical examinations. All 182 yielded the eight MRI features and entered the common comparison of M0, M1, MRI and Fusion. The remaining 56 examinations from 41 patients were not covered by the accessed mirror and comprised 46 tumor and 10 treatment-related-change examinations. These records remained in the coverage ledger and did not enter the imaging comparison. The imaging cohort included 141 tumor examinations (77.5%) and 41 treatment-related-change examinations (22.5%). At the patient level, 114 patients contributed tumor examinations and 29 contributed treatment-related-change examinations; eight patients contributed examinations in both categories. These category-specific patient counts therefore overlapped. Table 1 summarizes the imaging cohort, and Supplementary Table S1 describes the retained clinical cohort according to imaging coverage. The same 182 examinations were retained for the primary comparison and the erosion sensitivity analysis.
