All 182 examinations retained complete MRI features after the prespecified erosion, allowing comparison on the original case set and fold assignments. The eroded MRI model had an OOF AUC of 0.765, compared with 0.762 for the original MRI model (Supplementary Table S4; Figure S1). Eroded Fusion had an AUC of 0.788 (95% patient-bootstrap percentile interval, 0.706 to 0.867), compared with 0.782 for original Fusion. The paired eroded-minus-original Fusion AUC difference was 0.0062, with an interval of -0.0051 to 0.0183 that included zero. Eroded Fusion had a Brier score of 0.141 and treatment-related-change AP of 0.529, and correctly recognized 14 of 41 treatment-related-change examinations at the fixed threshold. The corresponding original Fusion values were 0.144, 0.505 and 12 of 41. These findings described sensitivity to one boundary perturbation under fixed processing and did not establish superiority of erosion or segmentation accuracy. The original ROI remained the primary analysis.
