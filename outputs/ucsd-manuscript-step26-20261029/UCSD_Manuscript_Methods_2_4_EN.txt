For each of the 182 included examinations, we used a FLAIR volume, a postcontrast T1-weighted volume and the accompanying standard BraTS mask, giving 546 compressed NIfTI files. File checks covered recorded byte counts and SHA256 digests, gzip integrity, three-dimensional array decoding, finite voxel values and valid header affines. Within each examination, the images and mask were required to have matching dimensions, spatial-unit fields and coded coordinate forms. Affine corner coordinates were compared using a tolerance of 0.000001 in the supplied coordinates. This established voxel-grid consistency for computation, without independently validating anatomical alignment. All files contained 256 by 256 by 256 arrays stored as unsigned 8-bit integers, and their spatial-unit fields were unknown. We analyzed the supplied derived voxel grids without additional registration or resampling and did not infer millimeter dimensions or physical volumes from their headers. Structural MRI intensities were treated as derived values. Quantitative diffusion measurements were not included.

The dataset authors describe standard BraTS annotations generated by automated segmentation, followed by radiologist correction and approval (1). Our analysis used the existing masks supplied with the mirror. For each examination, the region of interest (ROI) was the union of all strictly positive integer labels in its standard BraTS mask. The same rule was applied to both diagnostic classes and retained every connected component, without selecting the largest region, filling holes or expanding boundaries. Masks containing negative, noninteger or nonfinite values, empty unions and masks covering the entire array were ineligible. All 182 included examinations yielded valid nonempty unions. The ROI was treated as the source annotation region, without assigning tissue-specific interpretations to individual numeric codes. The separate cellular tumor reference masks were not used. We did not train a segmentation model or revise the supplied contours. Our union operation did not read the diagnostic label; blinding of the upstream annotation process to reference diagnostic information could not be verified.
